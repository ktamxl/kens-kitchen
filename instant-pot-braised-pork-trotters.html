Instant Pot Braised Pork Trotters

无水红烧猪蹄
Serves4 with rice
Prep~15 minutes
PressureMeat/Stew · 45 min
DifficultyEasy

Wú shuǐ — waterless. The whole point of this braise is that not a drop of water goes in: a full cup of Huadiao wine carries the liquid, and the trotters, soy, oyster sauce and rock sugar do the rest under pressure. Forty-five minutes in the Instant Pot on Meat/Stew turns the hardest-working cut in the kitchen into something gelatinous, sticky and lacquered, and then a final few minutes of reduction gives it the shine you see on the plate. Set it going and walk away.

01

Ingredients

The Pot

Pork trotters, cut into pieces1000 g
Rock sugar (冰糖)35 g
Ginger, sliced30 g
Garlic cloves6
Star anise4
Dried red chilies6
Bay leaves2
Cinnamon stick1 small piece
Minced garlic1 tsp
Have the butcher cut them. Trotters come whole and they're awkward to break down at home — ask for them split into 4–5 cm pieces. You want the skin and the collagen, not dainty meat.

The Seasoning

Light soy sauce (生抽)2 tbsp
Dark soy sauce (老抽)1 tbsp
Oyster sauce1 tbsp
Huadiao / Shaoxing cooking wine (花雕酒)1 cup
White peppera pinch
Vinegar½ tsp
Salt¼ tsp
Sugar1 tsp
No water — that's the recipe. The cup of Huadiao is the entire braising liquid. Don't be tempted to top it up; the trotters release plenty of their own liquid under pressure.
02

Instructions

  1. 1
    Blanch the Trotters
    Put the trotters in a pot of cold water and bring it to a boil. Once it's boiling, cook for 5 minutes, then lift them out and rinse each piece under running water, rubbing off any scum.
    ⚠️ Don't skip the rinse. That grey scum is what turns a braise cloudy and smells of the butcher's counter.
  2. 2
    Preheat the Pot
    Set the inner pot into the Instant Pot and heat it up — 烤热锅 / preheat — then switch to Sauté.
  3. 3
    Aromatics and Spices
    Add the trotters, then the ginger, garlic, star anise, bay leaves, dried chilies, cinnamon and the minced garlic. Toss everything together so the spices sit against the meat.
  4. 4
    Stir-Fry 8 Minutes
    Keep it on Sauté and stir-fry for 8 minutes — 翻炒8分钟. You're not trying to brown the meat so much as wake up the spices and drive the raw edge off the aromatics.
  5. 5
    Mix the Sauce — No Water
    Pour in the dark soy (1 tbsp), then the oyster sauce (1 tbsp) and the vinegar (½ tsp), and stir to coat. Add the light soy, rock sugar, salt and white pepper, then pour in the full cup of Huadiao wine. Stir, scraping the base of the pot.
  6. 6
    Pressure Cook 45 Minutes
    Lid on, valve to Sealing. Press Meat/Stew and set it for 45 minutes (炖煮功能键 45分钟). Let the pressure come down naturally before opening the lid.
    ⚠️ Natural release, not quick release. A fast release pulls steam through the sauce and can leave the skin tough instead of gelatinous.
  7. 7
    Reduce to a Glaze
    Open the pot, taste, and add the teaspoon of sugar. Turn on Sauté and reduce — tossing the trotters through the sauce — until it's thick, dark and glossy and coats each piece. Don't walk away here; it goes from glaze to sticky in a minute.
  8. 8
    Serve
    Lift the pieces out with chopsticks, spoon the glaze over, and scatter with shredded scallion. Steamed jasmine rice underneath, and something sharp on the side — the trotters are rich.
03

Chef's Notes

🍶

Waterless Means Wine

One cup of Huadiao is the whole braising liquid. Shaoxing works if you can't find Huadiao — but skip the cheap "cooking wine" with salt in it, it will make the glaze taste harsh.

⏱️

45 Minutes Is a Guide

Meat/Stew at 45 minutes gives a firm, sliceable trotter. If you want them collapsing — the jelly-like texture — go 60 minutes. The pot doesn't care; the texture does.

🥢

Why Trotters Are Worth It

Almost all skin and tendon, so an hour of pressure turns the collagen into gelatin — the reason the sauce sets like jelly when it cools and why it tastes so rich with so little meat.

🍚

Next Day

Leftovers keep 3–4 days in the fridge and set into a jelly — reheat gently with a splash of wine or stock. Any leftover glaze is superb spooned over rice or noodles.

Scaling This Recipe

Roughly 250 g of trotters per person with rice. The pressure time doesn't change unless you change the pot size.
